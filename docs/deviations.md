# Deviations from the registered design

Log every departure from docs/design.md here with the date and the reason
(design document, Section 11). Leave the table empty until a deviation occurs.

|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-12 | 3 | The secondary graduation outcome runs through school year 2020-21, not 2023-24. Its window is end years 2011 through 2021. | LEA-level adjusted cohort graduation rate files exist only through 2020-21. The ED Data Library's graduation listings for 2022-23 and 2023-24 hold state-level files only, and a state-level rate cannot form a within-district gap (Section 6). Consequence: reforms after 2020 have no post-reform years in either outcome and remain not-yet-treated controls. |
| 2026-09-12 | 3 | School year 2019-20 is retained for the graduation outcome. It stays excluded for the achievement gaps. | The Section 3 exclusion of 2019-20 applies to the waived assessments; graduation rates were reported that year, and the LEA file exists. |
| 2026-09-12 | 5 (rules 1, 2) | Graduation outcome: rules 1 and 2 are applied over end years 2010-2021, 2009-10 included. From 2014-15 the CCD LEA Directory has no `TYPE` or `BOUND`; `LEA_TYPE` (1-2 regular) stands in for `TYPE` and `UPDATED_STATUS` for `BOUND`, under the same codes (2, 6, 7 not operational; 5, 8 a change). | Author decision. v18 does not say how rule 2 reads the post-2014 CCD fields or which years the graduation stability test covers. `UPDATED_STATUS` uses `BOUND`'s codes 1-8; 2009-10 is included because the covariates and SAIPE rate are fixed there. `LEA_TYPE` 9 (specialized district, new in 2019-20) fails rule 1. |
| 2026-09-12 | 5 (rule 6) | Graduation outcome: rule 6 uses each event table's groups unchanged (excluded = a reform in 2005-2009), with cohorts coded over end years 2011-2021, so a state treated in 2010 or 2011 has no graduation pre-period and is left out. | Author decision. The graduation window starts a year after the achievement window, and v18 gives no separate exclusion rule for it; re-deriving exclusion for 2006-2010 would need treatment years the blinded tables do not supply for excluded states. |
| 2026-09-12 | 7 | Graduation outcome: the tested-count robustness weight is the 2010-11 cohort count in the gap's two groups, fixed across years; a district without a 2010-11 row is left out of the weighted models only. | Author decision. There is no 2009-10 graduation file, so the Section 7 weight (students tested in 2009-10) has no graduation counterpart; the first graduation year is the nearest fixed count. |
| 2026-09-12 | 7 | Graduation outcome: the regression-based secondary estimators control for CEP (district-year) and the four 2009 covariates interacted with year, without the test-replacement flag; step 6 runs them on all three event sets. | Author decision. The test-replacement flag describes the assessment regime and has no bearing on a graduation rate. Section 6 gives the graduation gaps the same event sets as the primary gaps. |
| 2026-09-12 | 4, 11 | The SY2018-19 (end year 2019) LEA achievement and participation files for mathematics and reading/language arts are taken from the Education Data Center EDFacts archive (`eddatacenter.org/edfacts`; ED's legacy long-layout files `*-lea-sy2018-19-long.csv`, `DATE_CUR` 13AUG20), not from ED's own ED Data Library release. | 2026-09-12 | 5 (rules 1, 2) | Achievement gaps: from 2014-15 the CCD LEA Directory's `LEA_TYPE` stands in for `TYPE` and `UPDATED_STATUS` for `BOUND`, under the same codes as the graduation outcome (2, 6, 7 not operational; 5, 8 a change; `LEA_TYPE` 9 fails rule 1). The design does not say how rule 2 reads the post-2014 CCD fields. Rule 2 requires presence "in every year of the window", and 2019-20 is outside the achievement window (Section 3). Including 2019-20 would exclude 5 more districts, 3 of them high school districts with a SAIPE 2009 rate, all in Montana. |
| 2026-09-12 | 5 (rule 4) | Exact-only suppression sample: a cell whose participation is reported as the band GE95 passes the participation rule, as an exact value of 95 or more or GE99 does. Six cells in the full window: district 3819410 (ND) in 2014-15, math and RLA, all students and White; district 1722110 (IL) in 2017-18, math and RLA, all students. Step 3's check now stops only on a participation band that straddles 95. | Author decision. The rule for that sample (decision log, 2026-09-11) keeps exact participation values and names exact values and GE99 as what its cells report; the full window adds GE95, which the stage 1 files did not contain. GE95 cannot lie below 95, so it passes under the midpoint reading and the lower-bound reading alike. |
| 2026-09-12 | 7 (secondary estimators) | Achievement gaps, regression-based secondary estimators: CEP enters gaps (b) and (c) as the district's own district-year flag (the step 3 `cep` column: the state phase-in table before end year 2014, the district's CCD `NSLPSTATUS` from 2014), with `test_replaced` attached by district-year. For gap (a), a state-year outcome, CEP is the share of that state-year's gap (a) districts under CEP: quintile 1 or 5, a valid 2009-10 membership, retained under the event set, and the all-students cell usable in both math and RLA under rules 3 and 4 in the suppression sample (`gap_a_flags()`). `test_replaced` stays state-year. | Author decision. The decision of 2026-09-11 treated both controls as state-year flags, which held while CEP came from the state phase-in table (stage 1). From 2014 the `cep` column is district-level (data acquisition 2.5), so it differs within a state-year and step 6 could not form a state-year flag. The graduation outcome already takes CEP by district-year (entry above). The share keeps gap (a)'s control on the districts that form the outcome. |
| 2026-09-13 | 7 | The secondary estimators move to the unbalanced panel. Sun-Abraham, the imputation estimator, the stacked regression and the two-way fixed effects comparison rows run on the step 5 unbalanced panel for the achievement gaps and the graduation gaps, and those results are the main step 6 files (`outputs/06_secondary/`, `outputs/06_secondary/graduation/`). Synthetic difference-in-differences stays at the state level: its state-year means are taken over the unbalanced panel's districts, and the states with a mean in every window year enter (the rest are left out and counted). The balanced-panel versions of every estimator are written to `outputs/06_secondary/appendix/` (graduation: `appendix/graduation/`). This replaces the statement of Section 7 that the secondary estimators run on the balanced panel. | At the full window the balanced panel holds 69 districts in 9 states for gap (b) and 35 districts in 12 states for gap (c), too few for the Section 13 agreement table to serve its purpose, which is to show whether the primary estimate survives other estimators' weighting and comparison choices on the same sample. The unbalanced panel is the primary estimator's sample, so the table now compares estimators rather than estimators and samples at once. synthdid cannot take an unbalanced state-by-year matrix; restricting it to states observed in every year is the smallest change that keeps it at the state level (author decision, 2026-09-13). |
| 2026-09-13 | 7 (code correction) | Imputation estimator on the unbalanced panel: the custom weights for the event-time and overall estimands are built over the treated observations whose unit has at least one untreated observation, and the number of treated units left out is recorded in the model notes (`run_imputation()`). The balanced-panel results are unchanged. | A code error exposed by the move to the unbalanced panel. A treated district observed only after its state's reform has no unit effect to impute; `didimputation` drops those rows and rescales the weights, so the overall was no longer the equal-weight mean of the event-time estimates that the decision of 2026-09-11 defines (it differed by 0.0002 SD for gap (b) and 0.007 SD for gap (c)). Found by the step 6 output check in `tests/test_secondary.R`. |
| 2026-09-13 | 8 (code correction) | Randomization inference, tie handling. The two-sided p-value counts a placebo estimate as at least as far from zero as the observed one when it is within a relative tolerance of 1e-9 of it (`ri_pvalue()`, `R/functions/inference.R`). Step 7 was rerun at the registered counts for both outcomes; the estimates and draws are unchanged, and the p-values rose by at most 0.010 (5 achievement models) and 0.033 (6 graduation models). | A code error, not a change of rule: Section 8 defines the p-value from the reassignments whose estimate is at least as far from zero. A reassignment that reproduces the observed treated states gives the observed estimate again, but the refit can land a floating-point hair below it, and the strict comparison then left such ties out, making the p-value too small. The ties are frequent where an event set has a single cohort. Found when `tests/test_inference.R` rebuilt the p-values from the saved draws. |
| 2026-09-13 | 5, 7, 8 (code correction) | Test updates for the full window. The output checks in `tests/` that assumed the four-year stage 1 window now use the full window: a balanced-panel unit has an outcome in all 11 window years (`ACH_WINDOW`), not 4; a step 5 model on a small balanced panel may carry `did`'s "no valid att_gt() estimates" error as its status, while every unbalanced model and every primary-set unbalanced model must fit; a Romano-Wolf family exists only where at least two of its gaps have a step 5 fit, and the primary-set unweighted families must exist; the exact-only sample's participation check stops on a band straddling 95 rather than on any band wider than 1 point (the GE95 entry above). Reader tests were added for the long EDFacts layout and the gap (a) CEP share. | The tests encoded properties of the stage 1 files, not rules of the design; the rules they check are unchanged. |
