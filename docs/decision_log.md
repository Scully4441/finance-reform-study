# Author decisions before registration

Decisions the author makes before the OSF registration is filed that change or add to
a rule in `docs/design.md` or depart from the committee-approved proposal. Section
numbers refer to the design document. The log closes when the registration is filed.
Departures from the registered design after filing go in `docs/deviations.md`.

| 2026-11-19 | 1.2, 12.3, 8.4 | Verdict rule for H1, H2 and H3, one verdict per gap: not evaluable when no bound set exists; criterion not evaluable when fewer than three of the four secondary estimators can be estimated; supported when the bound set at M̄ = 1 lies wholly above zero and the agreement criterion holds; contradicted when it lies wholly below zero and the criterion holds; not supported otherwise. | 2026-11-19 | 12.2 | Agreement criterion: each of the four secondary estimators gives an overall average that has the same sign as the primary estimate and lies inside the primary estimate's 95 percent bootstrap interval, and so does each registered sample and weighting variant that exists for the family. | 2026-11-19 | 12.3, 8.4 | H4 is evaluated per gap with the growth contrast G, the mean of the estimated event-time effects +4 to +8 minus the mean of 0 to +3, with its bootstrap interval, a Romano–Wolf adjustment across the three gaps, and relative-magnitudes bound sets on the contrast.
