reason (docs/design_extension.md, Section 13). The log is opened when the Run 2
registration is filed. Leave the table empty until a deviation occurs. Decisions
made before filing are in docs/decision_log_run2.md; Run 1's logs are
docs/decision_log.md and docs/deviations.md.

| 2026-09-16 | 4, 5 | Idaho 2022-2024 enter from the legacy SDE ISAT workbooks, archived from Wayback Machine copies (`hs_reportcard_id_sde`; captures 2022-11-08, 2024-06-01 and 2025-08-30, archived url and capture date in each row's `source`), not from the Idaho Report Card export, whose rows `hs_reportcard_id` 2022-2024 stay unavailable. They publish rates with the count omitted for small groups, which is the case Section 5 covers by the state's minimum group size. |
